Privacy Policy

PawTrack: Pet Health Log

1. Introduction

PawTrack: Pet Health Log ("PawTrack", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application developed by Matheus Pegoraro. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our app.

By using PawTrack, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Account Information (optional):

  • Email address and full name — only if you choose to create an account
  • If you use the app without creating an account, an anonymous identifier is generated automatically

Pet Information (user-provided):

  • Pet name, species, breed, birth date, weight, and microchip ID
  • Health records: vaccines, medications, vet visits, symptoms, and weight logs
  • Pet photos uploaded by you

Device Information (automatic):

  • Push notification token — to send you health reminders
  • Device platform (iOS or Android)

Subscription Information:

  • Subscription status (free or premium) — managed by RevenueCat
  • We do not collect or store payment details — all payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core features (health records, reminders)
  • To send push notifications about upcoming vaccines, medications, and vet appointments
  • To sync your data across devices when you create an account
  • To manage your premium subscription status
  • To generate PDF health reports when you request them

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database platform hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure in the South America (São Paulo) region.

We implement the following security measures:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) — your data is only accessible to your authenticated account
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data transmission
  • Authentication tokens stored securely on your device using platform-native secure storage

5. Third-Party Services

PawTrack uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Privacy Policy
  • Expo — push notification delivery. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — payment processing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

  • When you explicitly choose to share a pet's profile with family members or pet sitters using the Family Sharing feature
  • When you export and share a PDF health report
  • As required by law or to protect our legal rights

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — all your data is visible within the app
  • Export your data — use the PDF export feature
  • Delete your account and all associated data — contact us at the email below
  • Opt out of push notifications — toggle in Settings or via device settings

8. Children's Privacy

PawTrack is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data (pet profiles, health records, reminders, photos) will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Anonymous accounts that have been inactive for more than 12 months may be automatically deleted.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and updating the "Effective Date" below. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
