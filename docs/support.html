Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No! PawTrack works without an account. An anonymous session is created automatically. You can optionally create an account to back up your data and sync across devices.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → PawTrack Pro → Cancel Subscription. Your premium access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple through their standard refund process. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us at matheuspegoraro@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Account" and we will permanently remove all your data within 30 days.

Is my pet's data private?

Yes. Your data is stored securely and is only accessible by you. We never sell or share your information. Read our full Privacy Policy.

Can I share my pet's profile with family?

Yes! With PawTrack Pro, you can share pet profiles with family members or pet sitters so everyone stays informed about your pet's health.
